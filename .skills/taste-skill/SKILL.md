# Anti-Slop Frontend Design Guide: Running Crew Edition

## 1. What We Reject (AI Clichés & Slop)
- **No purple/indigo gradient backdrops** or generic AI glow effects.
- **No cookie-cutter 3-card feature grids** with generic icon badges in pastel rounded squares.
- **No hollow placeholder copy or vanity stats** ("99.9% Happy Runners", "10M+ Kilometers").
- **No sterile corporate minimalism** pretending to be sport culture.
- **No em-dashes (—) or robotic marketing prose.**

## 2. What We Build (Authentic Street Running Culture)
- **Vibe:** Urban, gritty, energetic, yet disciplined like Tracksmith meets Nike Running Division.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Base: High-contrast Dark Charcoal (`#0F1115`) & Off-Black (`#161920`) or Crisp Alabaster/Slate.
  - Accent: High-visibility Volt Lime (`#D4F932` or `#CCFF00`) and Racing Orange (`#FF5500`) for badges, paces, and interactive callouts.
  - Neutral borders: Refined muted slate (`#262B35`), clean division lines, tangible typography.
- **Typography & Hierarchy:**
  - Dynamic display headings with tight tracking (`font-black tracking-tight`).
  - Monospace accents for running metrics: `5'15" /km`, `10.2 km`, `48:32`, `Elev +142m`.
- **Tangible Running Crew Data:**
  - Real meeting spots (e.g. 남산 국립극장 입구, 반포 한강공원 달빛광장, 서울숲 야외무대).
  - Concrete pace groups: Group A (Sub-4:30), Group B (5:00-5:30), Group C (6:00-6:30 Recovery).
  - Real RSVPs with member status, crew check-ins, and admin approval workflows.

## 3. Human Experience Details
- Fast, tactile interactions with instantaneous feedback (toast, optimism, crisp badges).
- Direct status indicators: `[승인 대기중 (Pending Approval)]`, `[크루원 정회원]`, `[크루 마스터 (Admin)]`.
- Purpose-built admin management drawers/tables with clear toggle buttons and audit log timestamps.
